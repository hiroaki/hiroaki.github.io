leaflet-pan-direction

A small Leaflet v2 library for deriving a geographic heading from the user's most recent map drag gesture.

Repository

Demo

  • examples/basic.html — compares several heading algorithms and visualizes their results.
  • examples/street-view.html — uses the most recent pan direction as the initial heading for Google Street View.

The Street View demo also allows the viewing direction to be adjusted after selecting a location: pan the Leaflet map again and the heading, direction indicator, and Street View link update while the selected location remains fixed.
